The time should not be taken at face value, as for many of the training runs (especially the WGAN), we ran multiple runs simultaneously. It is realistic to expect here around a 6.5x speedup (i.e. WGAN takes 6.5x longer than diffusion) as a baseline estimate (WGAN has 2 neural networks to train, and here we use n_critic = 5 which seems to perform much better than n_critic = 1). In other words, for each minibatch, WGAN does 6 gradient steps compared to diffusion's single step (the critic steps also require heavier computation due to the critic penalty, thus the 6.5x instead of 6x factor). 

We also point out here that diffusion did not use EMA, and likely would have better metrics if used. (We did not find this experiment particularly interesting, thus the lack of experimentation).
